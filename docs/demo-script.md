# Fetola demo script (Mon 30 Nov 2026)

**Story:** Mama Tee Spaza in Gugulethu. Thandeka records her day in plain words. Every tap becomes a proper double-entry journal entry, and stock and petty cash look after themselves.

**Before you start (morning of the demo)**
1. Open the demo link on the laptop you will present from, in a full-width browser window. You get the desktop layout: a sidebar on the left, forms in a centred column, and Check and save as a dialog. On a phone the same steps work with the bottom bar.
2. Click **Reset demo** at the bottom of the sidebar (on a phone: **menu, top right → Reset demo**), then **Yes, reset the demo**. The shop gets about 8 weeks of history up to yesterday, and Home's "Today" list is empty.
3. Check **Stock** in the sidebar: **Cooldrink 2L shows 4 bottles, running low**. Every number below starts from there.
4. **Guided tour (optional):** a card walks through these same steps and moves on as you save each one. It starts on first visit; to run it again: **Demo settings → Restart tour** (it offers to reset first). Switch it off in Demo settings, or open the link with `?tour=off`, if you'd rather present from this script.

> Cooldrink 2L costs **R22.00** and sells for **R30.00**. The petty cash and bank totals depend on the day, so read them off the screen. On Monday 30 Nov nothing is owed to the supplier beforehand, so "You owe the supplier" reads exactly R176.00.

---

### 1. Buy stock from a supplier, pay later
**Home → Buy stock** (header button or tile) **→ Cooldrink 2L**
- Tap **+** until Quantity reads **8**. Cost each fills in as **R22.00** and the Total shows **R176.00**.
- Supplier: **Gugulethu Cash & Carry** · How are you paying? **Pay later**. The strip reads "Stock after this: 4 → 12 bottles".
- **Review purchase**. The sheet shows: Stock goes up 4 → 12 bottles · You owe the supplier R176.00 · Petty cash and bank: No change.
- **What your books record:** Dr 1200 Inventory 176.00 / Cr 2000 Trade creditors 176.00, marked **Balanced**. Tap **Save purchase**.
- *Say:* "She paid nothing today, so her cash doesn't move. The business now owes the supplier. That's a creditor, not an expense."
- *Optional:* choose **Petty cash** or **Bank** to show "paid now". To settle later: tap the "You owe Gugulethu Cash & Carry" row, or **You owe suppliers** on Home.

### 2. Sell that item at its list price
**Home → New sale** (header button, or the green Sell tile) **→ Cooldrink 2L** → **+** to **2**. The big figure reads **R60.00** ("2 × Cooldrink 2L at R30.00 (list price)").
- Paid with **Cash**. The panel shows Stock 12 → 10, What these cost you **R44.00**, Profit on this sale **R16.00** with a **27% margin** pill.
- **Review sale → Save sale.** Books: Dr 1010 Cash on hand 60.00 / Cr 4000 Revenue 60.00, and Dr 5000 Cost of sales 44.00 / Cr 1200 Inventory 44.00.
- *Say:* "Profit on every sale, at weighted average cost, without her ever hearing the words 'cost of sales'."

### 3. A related expense: packaging
**Home → Expense** → What was it for? **Packaging** → How much **85** → Description: tap **Carrier bags** under the field → **Petty cash** → **Review expense → Save expense**.
- Books: Dr 6600 Packaging materials 85.00 / Cr 1010 Cash on hand 85.00.

### 4. Count stock: 10 → 8, lost or stolen
**Home → Count** → Cooldrink 2L: tap **−** twice. It reads **8**, with "Expected 10 · 2 short" in red. Keep **Lost or stolen**. The dark card shows **R44.00 stock loss**.
- **Save count**, then **Save count** in the sheet. Books: Dr 5100 Stock losses 44.00 / Cr 1200 Inventory 44.00.
- *Optional:* the other reasons. **Used in the shop** posts to 6700 Consumables and office supplies; **Taken for own use** posts to 2100 Shareholder loan. Neither is cost of sales, because nothing was sold.

### 5. Petty cash
**Petty cash** in the sidebar: the balance on the left, and on the right a table of every movement, grouped by day, with In, Out and the balance after each.
- **Top up → 500 → Review top-up → Save top-up.** The sheet says money in or out of the business: No change. It's just a move. **Bank it** does the reverse.
- *Optional:* **Take for yourself** records the owner taking cash out: Dr 2100 Shareholder loan / Cr 1010 Cash on hand. The sheet says plainly when she will then owe the business.

### 6. Reports
**Reports**: Day / Week / Month and the arrows sit in the header. Three tiles (Sales, Expenses, Profit), then the sales chart (the green bar is the current day or week) beside the breakdown.
- The breakdown reads: Sales − Cost of what you sold (including the R44.00 stock lost) − Expenses = Profit.
- This week can show a small loss because the stolen stock is a real cost. **Month → back arrow** shows a full month with a profit.
- *Optional:* **Balance sheet** at the top switches to what the business owns, what it owes and her stake, as at today, with the check that both sides agree.

### 7. Accountant view (the pricing story)
**Books** in the sidebar. On a laptop the selected entry sits beside the table; on a phone it opens full screen.
- **Today** lists exactly the five entries from steps 1–5. Click one to see its reference, journal table, "Debits equal credits", where it was recorded from, who recorded it, and "Can it be edited? No · reverse it instead".
- The KPI tiles show today's entries, total debits = total credits, and the trial balance **Balanced**.
- In the tabs along the top: **Trial balance** (also shows that the stock records tie to 1200 Inventory), **Profit and loss**, **Balance sheet**, and **Exports** (CSV for Xero, Sage or Excel).
- *Say:* "This is real double-entry bookkeeping, not a cash book. An accountant can sign off on these books, which is why this costs more than a spreadsheet and less than a bookkeeper."

### More to show if there are questions (optional)
- **Customers who owe you:** tap **Customers owe you** on Home. Lwazi Crèche owes R152.00 on INV-0001. Record the payment (EFT, into the bank): Dr 1000 Bank / Cr 1100 Trade debtors, and Home drops to R0.00.
- **Back-dating:** on **Expense** or **Buy stock**, change the **Date** to a day last week (up to 60 days back). Check and save shows "Dated …", and the entry lands on that day in Books.
- **Sending an invoice:** **Invoice a customer**, add a WhatsApp number (or leave it empty), then **Save and send on WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the invoice text filled in.

### What's next (optional): bank statement preview
Turn on **Demo settings → Bank statement (preview)** beforehand, or open the link with `?bank=on`. **Bank statement** appears in the sidebar with a Preview label.
- **Use sample statement (Capitec Business, last 14 days).** It reads for a moment, then shows most lines already **Matched** to her books (tap one to open its journal entry).
- Three lines need her: a **R65.00 bank fee**, **R3.12 interest** and an **EFT from Lwazi Crèche for INV-0001**. Tap **Confirm** on each. The page then reads **Bank reconciled: statement R… = books R…**, and Home's "Customers owe you" drops to R0.00.
- *Say:* "Coming in the pilot: she uploads a statement from any bank and the app matches it to her records. Today this is a preview with a sample statement."

---

**If something goes wrong:** Reset demo (sidebar, or the phone menu), and run steps 1–5 again (about 2 minutes). Nothing leaves the device. The demo saves in this browser only, so practise and present on the same device and browser, or reset on the new one.
