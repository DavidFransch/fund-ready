# Fetola demo script (Mon 30 Nov 2026)

**Story:** Mama Tee Spaza in Gugulethu. Thandeka records her day in plain words. Every tap becomes a proper double-entry journal entry, and stock and petty cash look after themselves.

**Before you start (morning of the demo)**
1. Open the demo link on the device you will present from. The phone, or a laptop with the window narrowed, both work.
2. Tap **⋮ (menu) → Reset demo → Yes, reset the demo**. The shop now has about 8 weeks of history up to yesterday, and "today" is empty.
3. Check that **My stock** shows **Sunflower oil 2L: 4 bottles (running low)**. Every number below starts from there.

> Amounts that are always the same: oil costs **R48** a bottle and sells for **R65**. The petty cash and bank totals depend on the day, so read them off the screen.

---

### 1. Buy stock from a supplier, pay later
**Home → I paid for something**
- What was it for? **Stock** → **Sunflower oil 2L** → tap **+** until it reads **8** ("Stock goes 4 → 12"). The total cost fills in as **R384**.
- Supplier: **Gugs Wholesalers** · Whose money paid? **Pay later**
- **Check and update stock**. Read the bullets out: "added to your stock (4 → 12)", "Stock is not an expense yet", "Nothing is paid today, you will owe Gugs…".
- Open **What your books record**: Dr 1200 Inventory R384 / Cr 2000 Trade creditors R384. **Yes, save it.**
- *Say:* "She paid nothing today, so her cash doesn't move. The business now owes the supplier. That's a creditor, not an expense."
- *Optional:* to show "paid now", choose **Petty cash** or **Bank** instead. To settle later: **I paid for something → Pay now** on the yellow "You owe Gugs" card.

### 2. Sell that item at its usual price
**Home → I sold something** → **Sunflower oil 2L** → **+** to **2** → price fills in as **R65** → **Cash** → **Check and save**
- Bullets: R130 into petty cash, stock 12 → 10, "They cost you R96, so you made R34 on this sale."
- Books: Dr 1010 Cash R130 / Cr 4000 Revenue R130, and Dr 5000 Cost of sales R96 / Cr 1200 Inventory R96.
- *Say:* "Profit on every sale, at weighted average cost, without her ever hearing the words 'cost of sales'."

### 3. A related expense: packaging
**I paid for something → Packaging** → How much **85** → Where **Plastics wholesaler** → **Petty cash** → **Check and save**
- Books: Dr 6600 Packaging materials R85 / Cr 1010 Cash on hand R85.

### 4. Count stock: 10 → 8, lost or stolen
**Stock (bottom bar) → Count my stock** → Sunflower oil: tap **−** twice (**8**, "2 missing") → keep **Lost or stolen** → the card shows **R96 stock loss** → **Update my stock → Yes, save it**
- Books: Dr 5100 Stock losses R96 / Cr 1200 Inventory R96. "Used in the shop" would post to cost of sales instead.

### 5. Petty cash
**Petty cash (bottom bar)**: the balance, "In today R130 / Out today R85", and every movement with the balance after it.
- **Top up → 500 → Check and save**: "moves from the bank to petty cash… not money in or out of the business, just a move."
- *Say:* "Cash sales put money in, cash expenses take it out, and a top-up or deposit is just a transfer."

### 6. Reports
**Reports**: **Day** shows today's four actions. **Week** and **Month** use the arrows to go back (try **Month → Last month** for a full month with profit).
- "How the profit is worked out": sales − cost of stock sold or lost = made on sales − other costs = profit.
- Note: this week can show a small loss because the R96 stolen stock is a real cost. That's the point.

### 7. Accountant view (the pricing story)
**Books (bottom bar)**, or tap any item in Recent, or **See it in the books** on the toast after saving.
- **Added in demo** lists exactly the entries from steps 1–5, each with account codes, Dr/Cr and a "Balanced" total.
- The top cards: total debits = total credits; **item-by-item stock ties to account 1200**; append-only history, and every action has a unique id, so a retry never posts twice.
- **Trial balance** tab: debits equal credits as at today. 2000 Trade creditors includes today's R384 to Gugs.
- *Say:* "This is real double-entry bookkeeping, not a cash book. An accountant can sign off on these books, which is why this costs more than a spreadsheet and less than a bookkeeper."

---

**If something goes wrong:** ⋮ → Reset demo and start again (about 2 minutes to re-run steps 1–5). Nothing leaves the device. The demo saves in this browser only, so practise and present on the same device and browser, or reset on the new one.
